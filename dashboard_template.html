What the data says

League SIP is versus for every other half-inning. A pitcher's SIP is mostly his ordinary run prevention in disguise: the split-half reliability of SIP is low, and the part of SIP that is not explained by his other innings shows no repeatable skill. Pitchers do slip a little with big leads (5+ runs): about points within the same pitchers.
